Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

👋 Introduction

Welcome to CricScore — your live cricket scoring companion. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the CricScore mobile application.

By using CricScore, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

CricScore is designed with privacy in mind. We collect only the minimum data required to provide cricket scoring functionality.

📊 Information We Collect

Data stored on your device (local only):

  • Match data you enter (team names, player names, scores)
  • Match history and statistics
  • App preferences and settings
  • Scheduled match information

Data sent to our server (optional features):

  • Match scores and statistics (when you tap "Save Match")
  • Player career statistics
  • Anonymous match data for AI coaching tips
We do NOT collect: personal identification, email addresses, phone numbers, payment information, or device identifiers.

📍 Location Data

CricScore requests location permission only for the weather feature. Location is used to show current weather conditions at your match venue.

  • Location is accessed only when you tap "Use My Location"
  • Location data is sent to Open-Meteo (free weather API) to fetch weather
  • We do NOT store your location data on our servers
  • You can deny location permission — the app works fully without it

🎙️ Microphone / Speech

CricScore uses text-to-speech (voice commentary) to announce ball-by-ball events during scoring. This feature:

  • Uses your device's built-in speech engine only
  • Does NOT record or transmit any audio
  • Does NOT access your microphone
  • Can be turned off at any time using the toggle on the scoring screen

🔔 Notifications

CricScore may send local notifications for:

  • Weather alerts for scheduled matches (e.g. rain warning)
  • Match day reminders for scheduled matches

You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings. We do NOT send marketing or promotional notifications.

🔒 Data Security

We take data security seriously:

  • All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
  • Match data stored on our server is not linked to any personal identity
  • We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties
  • Our server is hosted on Railway (railway.app) with industry-standard security

🌐 Third Party Services

CricScore uses the following third-party services:

🌤️ Open-Meteo — Weather data (no account required)
🚂 Railway — Server hosting
📱 Expo — App framework

Each service has its own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing them if you have concerns.

👶 Children's Privacy

CricScore does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is designed for cricket players and enthusiasts of all ages. Since we collect no personal information, the app is safe for all age groups.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
